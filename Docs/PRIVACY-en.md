# Market Listem Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Market Listem is an on-device shopping-list application that does not require a user account or an external server.

## Data collection

Market Listem does not collect personal data, share data with third parties, or track users. Products, quantities, stock states, and purchase history are stored only on the user's device.

## Widget and local storage

An Apple App Group container is used to display the current shopping list in the Home Screen widget. This information is not sent off the device.

## Analytics and advertising

The application contains no advertising, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking.

## Deleting data

Products can be archived inside the application. Removing the application from the device also removes its local application data.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact channel listed on the application's App Store support page.
