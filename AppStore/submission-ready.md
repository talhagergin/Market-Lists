• Satın alma geçmişinden ürünleri yeniden ekle.
• Ana ekran widget'ından güncel market listeni kontrol et.

### Description

Market List is a focused shopping companion that helps you track everyday household products and move through the grocery store faster.

• Track products as In Stock, Running Low, or Out.
• Automatically move out-of-stock products to your shopping list.
• Set quantities and browse a list organized by category.
• Check off purchases in Shopping Mode.
• Add products again from purchase history.
• See your current shopping list in a Home Screen widget.

Market List requires no account. Your data stays on your device, with no advertising or user tracking.

### Review Notes

Market List is an offline grocery inventory and shopping-list app. No account or sign-in is required. On first launch, complete the short onboarding flow. Tap the plus button to add a product, set its quantity and stock status, and save it. Products marked as Out automatically appear in the shopping list. Shopping Mode lets the reviewer mark products as purchased. The Home Screen widget is optional and displays the current shopping list after it is added by the user. The app contains no advertising, analytics, subscriptions, in-app purchases, external APIs, or user tracking. All data remains on the device. The interface supports Turkish and English according to the device language.
